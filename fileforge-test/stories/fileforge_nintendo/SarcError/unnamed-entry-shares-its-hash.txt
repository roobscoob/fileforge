i Unnamed SARC entry collides (fileforge_nintendo::sead::sarc::error::SarcError<
                               '_, fileforge::storybook::StoryStream>)
  ❯ Entry 2 shares its hash with another entry, and one of them has no name.
  ❯ An entry can only leave out its name when no other entry shares its hash.
